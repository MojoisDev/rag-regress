# Billing support guide

## Invoices and receipts

Billing administrators download invoices from **Billing > Invoices**. Each
monthly invoice is available after the payment is posted, and the page retains
the most recent 24 months of invoices. Selecting an invoice opens a PDF-ready
view and a CSV line-item export. A payment receipt is separate from an invoice
and is available from the payment detail page. Support can resend a receipt to
the billing contact but cannot change the legal name on an issued invoice.

## Refund requests

Requests for a duplicate charge must be submitted within 30 days of the
charge. The billing team verifies the transaction ID, account ID, and charge
date before approving a refund. Approved refunds return to the original
payment method in five to ten business days. Subscription cancellations stop
future renewals but do not automatically refund a completed billing period.
Escalate card disputes, suspected fraud, or a refund delayed beyond ten
business days to Billing Escalations.

## Payment failures

When a payment fails, the billing administrator can update the payment method
and retry the invoice from the Billing page. The service retries a failed
renewal twice over seven days before the subscription is suspended. Do not ask
support to collect card numbers. If a bank transfer is pending for more than
three business days, include the invoice number in a Billing Escalations
request.
